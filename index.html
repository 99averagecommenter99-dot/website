Percentages · about 2 minutes

A $40 textbook is marked down by 15%. With 13% tax, what do you pay?

Original sample · not an official contest question
Apply the discount first. Then add tax to the discounted price—not the original price.
One step at a time:
  1. 15% off means pay 85%: $40 × 0.85 = $34.
  2. Add 13% tax: $34 × 1.13 = $38.42.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Which Waterloo contests can I prepare for?

Stepwise is focused on the Beaver Computing Challenge, Pascal, Cayley, and Fermat Contests, the Canadian Computing Competition, and the Euclid Contest. Their grade ranges are shown in the contest lineup.

Can I practice if I only have a few minutes?

Yes. Start with one problem, ask for a hint if you need one, and work through the steps at your own pace. You can come back when you have more time.

Are the sample questions official Waterloo contest questions?

No. The warm-up on this page is an original example to show how step-by-step practice works. It is not an official contest question.

How do I register for a Waterloo contest?

The school details shared for these contests say to visit Sr. Nejah in the computer lab. Check with your school for the latest registration steps and dates.
